# Mandala Garden 🌸

**A WWDC Swift Student Challenge Submission**

Mandala Garden is an interactive iOS/iPadOS application built with SwiftUI and SpriteKit that allows users to create beautiful, culturally-rich Mandala art. The app provides a serene, mindful experience where users can express their emotions and find relaxation through the ancient practice of Mandala creation.

## 📖 The Story Behind the App

The inspiration for Mandala Garden comes from a very personal place. I have someone I admire deeply who hails from the vibrant state of **Rajasthan, India**. It was through her that I was first introduced to the mesmerizing world of **Mandala art**. Her passion for this traditional art form truly inspired me, not just by its beauty, but by its deep emotional resonance. 

In our conversations and through observing her art, I learned a beautiful concept: **different flowers in a Mandala can represent different moods**. This idea—that we can channel our feelings, calm our minds, and reflect our inner state through the careful placement of flowers in a symmetric pattern—became the core philosophy of this app. Mandala Garden is a tribute to her, to the rich heritage of Rajasthan, and to the universal human need for emotional expression and mindfulness.

## 🛠 Why This App for WWDC?

For my WWDC submission, I wanted to build an app that sits at the intersection of **technology, culture, and mental well-being**. Here is how Mandala Garden stands out:

1. **Mindfulness & Mental Health:** Apple has always championed health and wellness. Mandala Garden is designed to be a digital safe haven. The repetitive, focused nature of creating a Mandala helps reduce anxiety and promotes a state of flow, offering users a therapeutic creative outlet.
2. **Expressing Emotions through Art:** By linking different flowers to different moods, the app goes beyond just being a drawing tool—it becomes an emotional journal. It allows users to visually map out how they are feeling on any given day.
3. **Showcasing Apple Technologies:**
   - **SwiftUI:** Used to build a fluid, beautiful, and responsive user interface that feels premium and native.
   - **SpriteKit:** Powers the interactive, physics-based, and high-performance canvas where the Mandala comes to life. It handles the dynamic placement and rotation of nodes perfectly.
   - **Concurrency:** Utilizing Swift's modern concurrency to handle data and state fluidly without dropping frames.
4. **Cultural Representation:** The app brings the beautiful aesthetic of Rajasthani art and traditional Mandalas to a global platform, showcasing how localized art forms can be translated perfectly into a modern digital experience using Apple's ecosystem.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Dynamic Mandala Canvas:** An interactive SpriteKit-powered canvas where users can drag, drop, and arrange flowers.
- **Mood-Based Flora:** A rich library of flower assets where each type of flower corresponds to a specific emotional state or mood.
- **Seamless UI:** A distraction-free, calming interface built entirely in SwiftUI.
- **Symmetrical Generation:** Automatic radial symmetry that ensures every creation looks mesmerizing and mathematically perfect.
